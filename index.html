Which classes does the school offer?

BBD International School is a UP Board school serving students from Nursery through Class 12. Please contact the school to confirm availability for a particular class and academic session.

How can I ask about admission?

Use the admission enquiry form on this page or call/WhatsApp the school at +91 99569 68794. Our team can guide you through the current process.

How do I get the fee details?

Fee details may depend on the class and academic session. Please contact the school office directly for the current, official fee information.

Where is the school located?

BBD International School is in Sansarpur, Bakshi Ka Talab (B.K.T.), Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh.
